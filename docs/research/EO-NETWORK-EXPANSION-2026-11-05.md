The section uses a page-owned random seed initialized after hydration. Filtering, language changes and changing the presentation preserve its order; a full reload or navigating away and returning creates another order. The browser regression checks both languages at mobile/desktop widths and verifies all portraits load, every profile remains available, filters retain relative order, new visits change it, and accessibility has no serious or critical findings.
