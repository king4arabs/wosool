# Saudi entrepreneurship source ledger — 8 October 2026

Scope: 18 bilingual organization, program and opportunity records for Wosool, plus EO Accelerator facts. Public primary sources only. Summaries are original; translations and taxonomy labels are editorial. This is a dated research snapshot, not live availability monitoring.

## Import and display rules

- Preserve `source_urls` and `verified_at` alongside imported fields. Identifiers, translations, categories and normalized tags are editorial mappings of the cited facts.
- `null` means not verified or not applicable; it is not zero, free participation, unrestricted eligibility or no deadline.
- `not_announced` means no current intake verified. For organizations and evergreen services, show “Check provider” rather than “Closed”. A generic registration button alone did not establish an open cohort.
- `open` is used only with an explicit dated enrollment statement or published ongoing service availability. Re-check it before time-sensitive outreach or application decisions.
- ISO dates are day precision. Do not invent deadline times or UTC offsets; display dates in the Saudi context without converting them to previous/next dates.
- `expired` records are archive entries. Exclude them from upcoming/open opportunities.
- No funding, acceptance, licensing, equity or membership outcome is guaranteed. Detailed provider terms control.
- None of these records proves a Wosool partnership. The unresolved RIDA label has no seeded identity or substituted logo.

## Unresolved identity and content conflicts

RIDA is not normalized to RDIA or Riyadah. Both are distinct, independently documented names; the requester must identify the intended organization. No RIDA record is published in the dataset.

- [RDIA official site](https://www.rdia.gov.sa/en/) confirms the Research, Development and Innovation Authority identity.
- [SDB Riyadah partner page](https://www.sdb.gov.sa/ar/our-partners/riyadah-center) confirms a separately named entrepreneurship institution.
- [TAQADAM interest form](https://share-eu1.hsforms.com/17Cg3qi_rQWyU_fV8Fzg99wezi8e) is linked as interest registration, not a dated admission call.
- [Wa’ed linked funding widget](https://edda-dealflow.waed.com/externalapi/submit-widget/embed/FsfsbswfxCkuDWDQC2qy/saudi_aramco) returned HTTP 502 to the retrieval tool; the official guidance page remains the linked destination.

Misk Launchpad calendar length and TAQADAM activity attendance headcount contain internal source inconsistencies. The ambiguous duration/headcount are deliberately absent from the public data fields. No expired event is rolled forward to 2027 or relabeled as upcoming.

Only read-only research was performed. No application, contact message, registration or external submission was sent.
