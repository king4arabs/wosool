# EO Riyadh Accelerator sources — 11 October 2026

This review supports the `/eoa` implementation. Source availability establishes an organization's identity or an asset's origin; it does not establish a partnership. Existing relationship approvals and editorial records are preserved by the insert-only `eoa:install` import.

## Program facts

Primary sources: [EO Accelerator](https://eonetwork.org/accelerator/) and [current FAQ](https://eonetwork.org/accelerator/faqs/), checked 11 October 2026.

| Topic | Verified content |
| --- | --- |
| Audience | Owners or founders of operating businesses seeking growth. |
| Revenue | US$250,000–999,999 gross yearly revenue. The FAQ also describes a privately raised funding route in that range for venture-backed applicants; local reviewers verify qualification. |
| Duration | EO describes a two-year learning program. |
| Rhythm | Four full-day learning sessions annually and monthly accountability groups. The FAQ estimates 8–12 group meetings, usually 2–4 hours per month, plus applying the learning. |
| Curriculum | Cash, Strategy, People, Execution. |
| Fees | US$1,750 annual global fee, plus locally determined fees. Proration applies. Local sponsorship and participant contributions require approved local terms. |
| Fiscal year | 1 July–30 June; intake timing varies by chapter. |
| Progression | Reaching US$1 million enables a separate EO application. Chapter review and acceptance still apply. |

Do not publish earlier local planning assumptions (nine months, SAR250,000 entry threshold, or a specific sponsorship split) as global EO requirements. The journey may present discovery, qualification, application, review, acceptance, onboarding, learning and completion, followed by a separate potential EO membership application; it must not promise revenue results or automatic membership.

## EO identity and chapter context

[EO's homepage](https://eonetwork.org/) currently reports approximately 20,568 members and Accelerator participants, 226 chapters and 66 countries. Some older chapter pages still show earlier totals. Prefer timeless copy about a global peer network unless figures carry their source date.

[EO Riyadh's fact sheet](https://member.eonetwork.org/riyadh/about-our-chapter) lists a 2013 founding and 26 chapter members as of 29 September 2026. The [chapter contact page](https://member.eonetwork.org/riyadh/contact-our-chapter) contains 2022/23 officer entries, so it cannot verify the current poster's board composition.
