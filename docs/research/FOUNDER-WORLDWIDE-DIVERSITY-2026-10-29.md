# Worldwide founder diversity — 29 October 2026

INTERNAL EDITORIAL DOCUMENT — not for the public website or mobile interface. Public cards must use concise bilingual biographies, sector and company information, authentic website/source links, and only verified rights-cleared media.

## Scope and approach

Starting point: 206 editorial founder profiles, 114 companies and 12 countries in three registered mirrored source catalog pairs. The present additive batch covers 35 previously unrepresented countries and introduces **70 public editorial founder profiles**, two per new country, raising the proposed source catalog to **276 profiles, 151 companies and 47 countries**, with news catalogs untouched (65 articles).

Country describes the **company's founding ecosystem or venture location**, not a founder's citizenship, birthplace, nationality or residency. Cross-border companies may be headquartered elsewhere today. Examples: M-KOPA operates in Nairobi but lists a London group headquarters, Intercom's Irish origin does not establish Irish incorporation, and an Israeli/Turkish regional exclusion rule remains unchanged. This source catalog is not a list of actual Wosool members or verified member accounts.

## Privacy, accuracy and deployment guardrails

- Keep public editorial records separate from actual user memberships and internal member scorecards: no account/user creation, invitations, outbound email, contact data or partnership endorsement.
- The new records have null portrait/logo and null traction fields. This is deliberate. Do not add unlicensed photographs, fabricated metrics or implied verification badges.
- The 70 new objects are append-only in `frontend/src/data/founder-spotlight-expansion-2026-10-22.json` and the exactly mirrored `backend/resources/data/founder-directory-expansion-2026-10-22.json`; older records are unchanged.
- Preserve `AGENTS.md`, `DEPLOYMENT.md`, baseline `a7be830`, frontend hoisted dependencies, runtime tracing and webpack limits. Run all frontend/backend integrity tests.
- The GitHub PR/merge is **not a production database import** and does not prove active Hostinger deployment. Retain production database, editorial modifications, private state, soft deletions and safe import permissions; verify live serving SHA independently. Do not automatically invoke the importer from CI.
