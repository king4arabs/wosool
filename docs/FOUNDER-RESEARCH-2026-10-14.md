# Founder research update — 14 October 2026

Added 14 public founder profiles at 11 companies. The shared frontend snapshot and database import now contain 43 founders at 33 companies. These are researched public profiles, not claimed accounts or verified Wosool members.

## Evidence policy

Each profile includes English and Arabic descriptions, role, sector, country, company website, reviewed date, source URLs, and a sourced traction statement. Founding years and personal LinkedIn links are included only where established. Missing portraits use the existing initials fallback.

Revenue is distinguished from funding, payment volume, downloads, customer counts and partnerships. Gameit’s founder reports sales revenue without an amount. Existing Swarm Robotics and Jahez records now include sourced revenue: Swarm’s Misk story does not specify a fiscal year; Jahez’s annual report is FY2025. These are attributed public statements, not independent financial audits.

CODE/Multiverse/Tech Founders connections are recorded for Gameit, Nugttah and Lahint; Misk connections for Lahint, Qawafel and EdfaPay; Monsha’at expansion-program connections for Rewaa and Ynmo; LEAP profiles for Heba Atyah, Bader Almubarak and Iyad Aldalooj; and Money20/20 participation for SiFi. Syarah adds a company with a public operating-volume announcement. No accelerator affiliation is inferred from geography.

## Deploy and import

Deploy the frontend and Laravel source from the same revision. Back up production using the normal deployment process, then run:

```sh
php artisan migrate --force
php artisan wosool:import-founders
php artisan wosool:import-news
```

The imports are additive and repeatable. On an installation containing the previous full research dataset, expected additions are 14 founders, 11 companies and 6 news items; existing matches may lower the created counts. Missing company traction/founding year is filled, while existing editorial content and claimed accounts are preserved. Repeated imports create no extra records. They create no users and send no invitations.

The fresh isolated SQLite validation database imported 43 founders, 33 companies and 40 news items, with zero users. Re-running both commands preserved all records and created none. Production import and live deployment require the hosting connection; this document does not assert they have run.
